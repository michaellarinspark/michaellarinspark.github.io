Personal household automation

My n8n Automations

A private-use automation operated by the owner of the GitHub account michaellarinspark. It reads household bill emails through Gmail and transfers bill details to the owner's Home Assistant dashboard.

What the automation does

The workflow uses n8n to process relevant billing messages and, where needed, attachments. It extracts details such as the provider, amount, due date, account reference and invoice number so household bills can be viewed in one place.

Why Gmail access is needed

Gmail authorization allows the configured workflow to retrieve bill messages and relevant attachments from the owner's connected mailbox. Access is limited by the permissions granted during Google authorization.

For personal use

This is not a public email service and does not accept customer sign-ups. This website contains information about the automation; it does not display bills or provide access to the mailbox or Home Assistant.
