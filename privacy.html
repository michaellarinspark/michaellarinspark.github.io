My n8n Automations

Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026.

Purpose and operator

My n8n Automations is a personal household bill-processing workflow operated by the owner of the GitHub account michaellarinspark.

Data accessed and used

The workflow accesses relevant Gmail message content and metadata and, when needed, bill attachments. It uses these to extract provider names, bill categories, amounts, due dates, account references, invoice numbers and instalment details. This information is used to display household bills in the owner's Home Assistant installation.

Processing and storage

Messages are processed by the owner's n8n installation. Extracted bill details are stored in Home Assistant text helpers and displayed through template sensors. Depending on n8n execution-history and backup settings, workflow inputs and outputs may also remain in execution records or backups. Original messages remain subject to the owner's Gmail retention settings.

Sharing and AI

The workflow does not send email content or PDFs to OpenAI or another AI service for extraction. Gmail data is used for the personal bill workflow and is not sold, used for advertising, or published on this website.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Retention depends on the owner's settings for Gmail, n8n, Home Assistant and backups; this policy does not promise a fixed automatic deletion period. The owner can delete extracted bill data from Home Assistant, remove n8n execution records and credentials, and manage retained backups. Deleting a displayed bill does not necessarily remove copies in history or backups.

Control over access

The owner can disconnect the Gmail credential in n8n or revoke the app's access through Google Account connections. Revoking access prevents subsequent authorized retrieval but does not delete previously stored data.

This website

This static site has no forms, analytics scripts or advertising and does not connect to Gmail. It is intended for hosting on GitHub Pages, whose hosting infrastructure may record visitor information such as IP addresses under GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Contact and changes

Use the support email displayed on the application's Google consent screen to contact the operator. This policy will be updated if the workflow's data practices change.
